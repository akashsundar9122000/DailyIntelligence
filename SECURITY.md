# Security

Please report a vulnerability privately, through GitHub's
[advisory form](https://github.com/akashsundar9122000/DailyIntelligence/security/advisories/new),
not in a public issue. Say what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it
could reach. You will get an answer within a week.

In scope: the application at https://daily-intelligence-gold.vercel.app and
this repository. Out of scope: the third-party services it uses (Neon, Vercel,
NVIDIA, the news sources and social platforms), which have their own
programmes; findings that need a compromised device; volumetric denial of
service.

Please do not test against other readers' accounts or data, and do not run
automated scanners against production: they trip the rate limits and cost the
service real money.
